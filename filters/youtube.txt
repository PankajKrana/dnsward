! Title: AdBlock YouTube compatibility rules
! Description: YouTube-specific rules, kept apart from the generic lists so
!   they can change without rebuilding AdBlock. YouTube changes often; to
!   receive fixes quickly, point the `youtube` list's `url` at a maintained
!   copy of this file (see docs/youtube.md).
! Expires: 1 day
! License: MIT
!
! How it works:
!   1. Network rules block ad-specific endpoints only. Video playback hosts
!      (googlevideo.com) are never blocked: ads and content share them.
!   2. Scriptlets remove ad placement data from player responses before the
!      player reads it (json-prune*), using built-in, reviewed extension
!      code. The rules below only supply property names.
!   3. Cosmetic rules hide ad containers in the page layout.
!   No DRM, authentication or access-control mechanism is touched.
!
! ---- Network: ad and ad-measurement endpoints ----
||youtube.com/api/stats/ads^
||youtube.com/pagead/
||youtube.com/ptracking^
||youtube.com/get_midroll_
||youtube.com/api/stats/qoe?*adformat
||googleads.g.doubleclick.net^
||static.doubleclick.net^
||imasdk.googleapis.com/js/sdkloader/ima3.js$domain=youtube.com|youtube-nocookie.com
!
! ---- Scriptlets: strip ad placements from player data ----
! Initial page data (inline script) and later navigations (fetch/XHR).
youtube.com,youtube-nocookie.com,youtubekids.com##+js(set-constant, ytInitialPlayerResponse.playerAds, undefined)
youtube.com,youtube-nocookie.com,youtubekids.com##+js(set-constant, ytInitialPlayerResponse.adPlacements, undefined)
youtube.com,youtube-nocookie.com,youtubekids.com##+js(set-constant, ytInitialPlayerResponse.adSlots, undefined)
youtube.com,youtube-nocookie.com,youtubekids.com##+js(json-prune, playerResponse.adPlacements playerResponse.playerAds playerResponse.adSlots adPlacements playerAds adSlots)
youtube.com,youtube-nocookie.com,youtubekids.com##+js(json-prune-fetch-response, playerAds adPlacements adSlots playerResponse.playerAds playerResponse.adPlacements playerResponse.adSlots, , propsToMatch, /\/(player|get_watch)\b/)
youtube.com,youtube-nocookie.com,youtubekids.com##+js(json-prune-xhr-response, playerAds adPlacements adSlots playerResponse.playerAds playerResponse.adPlacements playerResponse.adSlots, , propsToMatch, /\/(player|get_watch)\b/)
! Feed / search ads delivered as renderer objects.
youtube.com##+js(json-prune-fetch-response, onResponseReceivedEndpoints.*.appendContinuationItemsAction.continuationItems.*.adSlotRenderer, , propsToMatch, /\/browse\b/)
!
